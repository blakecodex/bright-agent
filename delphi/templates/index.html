How to read Delphi

verdictfairly priced within the band · overpriced above it · underpriced below it · insufficient data when the evidence is thin
confidencehow much evidence, how well it agrees, how far from the line. Below 0.55 no call is issued.
routeauto — the numbers stand on their own · analyst — a person reviews with the evidence attached
compsmedian of comparable closed sales; more comps, more weight (saturates at 12)
bandthe tolerance around the anchor that counts as fair; the market sets its width
modela second vote from a statistical model; its weight falls when its two halves disagree or the zip is unfamiliar
$/sqft checka size sanity check that can lower confidence, never move the price
domdays on market — the market's speed, and a signal about this listing
months of supplyhow long the current inventory would take to sell at the current pace
critica second pair of eyes on the verdict's story; a flag routes to an analyst

Trust and risk

  • Fair housing. Property and market facts only. No demographic feature or filter anywhere in the pipeline.
  • Appraisal independence. Delphi advises a licensed professional. It does not set a price.
  • Provenance. Every number traces to a public source with a timestamp and a hash.
  • Guardrails. Inputs are bounded, tool output is scanned for instruction-like text before any model reads it, and the answer shape is checked.
  • Nothing stored. Requests are not logged to disk here; the trace is returned to you and discarded.
  • Deterministic by default. The verdict is code, not a model's opinion. A model, when enabled, only plans and narrates.

Data

salesCity of Philadelphia, Office of Property Assessment — recorded deeds via the public Carto SQL API. Not MLS data: no list prices or listing DOM.
marketRedfin Data Center, county market tracker — 13 Bright-footprint counties, monthly. Data from Redfin.
modelHedonic ridge + one-hidden-layer MLP on log price, 5-fold validated against scikit-learn and PyTorch.
Delphi is decision support for licensed real-estate professionals. It never issues a verdict without showing its evidence, and a person makes the final call.
