# the bright mls footprint

Bright MLS serves the Mid-Atlantic: the District of Columbia and parts or all of Maryland, Virginia, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware and West Virginia. Philadelphia County, Montgomery County (PA), Baltimore County, Montgomery County (MD), Fairfax County and the District of Columbia are among its largest markets by transaction volume.

A pricing tool built for MLS subscribers serves agents and brokers first. The output that helps them is a defensible number with its evidence attached: the comps, the market backdrop, and a confidence they can explain to a seller at the kitchen table.

An automated verdict is advice, never a decision. Fair-housing and appraisal-independence rules mean a pricing recommendation must rest on property and market facts only, must be explainable on request, and must leave the final call with a licensed professional. The agent's route-to-analyst gate exists for that reason, not only for low confidence.
