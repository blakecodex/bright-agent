# glossary

Comp: a comparable closed sale used as evidence of value. Same zip, same beds, recent.

Median: the middle value of a sorted list. Robust to one extreme sale in a way the mean is not.

Hedonic regression: a model that treats a home as a bundle of attributes and estimates how much each attribute contributes to price, usually in log terms so effects are percentages.

Assessed value: the city's estimate of market value for tax purposes. Public for every parcel, updated periodically, and a strong but imperfect predictor of sale price.

Price per square foot: sale price divided by livable area. Useful for comparing homes of similar size, misleading across very different sizes.

Confidence: a number between zero and one that summarises evidence volume and agreement. It is not a probability that the verdict is correct; it is a signal for when a human should look.

Route to analyst: the agent's human-in-the-loop decision. Triggered by low confidence, missing evidence, or a critic flag on the verdict's internal consistency.
