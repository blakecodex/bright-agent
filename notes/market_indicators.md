# reading the market indicators

Months of supply is the inventory of active listings divided by the monthly sales pace. It answers "if nobody listed another home, how long until everything sold?" Under four months favours sellers, four to six is balanced, over six favours buyers. Philadelphia County sat near four to five months through spring 2026.

Median days on market (DOM) is how long the typical closed listing took to go under contract. A falling DOM means demand is heating; a rising DOM means it is cooling. A single listing whose DOM is more than one and a half times the market median is being told something by buyers, and the verdict nudges toward overpriced.

Sale-to-list ratio is the average closed price divided by the final list price. Above one means bidding wars; below one means negotiation. Philadelphia averaged just under 0.99 in 2026, meaning buyers were winning small discounts.

Inventory counts active listings at month end. Rising inventory with flat sales is the earliest sign of a shift toward buyers.

Redfin publishes these county figures monthly, but the most recent month is often partial because closings are still being recorded; the agent drops a trailing month whose sales count is far below the trailing median before reading it.
